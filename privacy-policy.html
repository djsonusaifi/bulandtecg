Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

At BulandTech, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and how we keep it safe when you use our website or services.

BulandTech आपकी गोपनीयता का सम्मान करता है। यह policy बताती है कि हम कौन सी जानकारी लेते हैं, क्यों लेते हैं और उसे कैसे सुरक्षित रखते हैं।

Valid ID firstA valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport) is required before any work starts.
70% advance70% of the quoted amount is paid in advance. Work begins only after it is received.
30% on deliveryThe remaining 30% is paid before final files, source code or live handover.

Information we collect / हम क्या जानकारी लेते हैं

We collect only what we need to deliver your project:

  • Contact details — name, phone, email and address
  • Identity proof — a copy or details of a valid government photo ID, collected before any work starts
  • Payment records — amount, date and transaction reference of advance and balance payments (we never store card or UPI PINs)
  • Project information — requirements, content, logos and files you share
  • Website usage — IP address, browser, device and pages visited

Why we collect your ID / ID क्यों ली जाती है

A valid ID is required before any work begins. It helps us confirm that orders are genuine, protects both parties from fraud and keeps a clear record of who the project belongs to.

कोई भी काम शुरू करने से पहले valid ID ली जाती है ताकि order genuine हो और fraud से दोनों पक्ष सुरक्षित रहें।

  • ID is used only for verification and billing records
  • It is never shared, sold or published
  • Access is limited to the BulandTech team handling your project
  • You may ask us to delete your ID copy once the project is complete and all payments are settled

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and send quotations
  • Verify identity and record advance (70%) and balance (30%) payments
  • Plan, build and deliver your project
  • Send project updates and support
  • Send offers — only with your permission

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers needed for your project (hosting, domain, app stores, ad platforms)
  • When required by law or a government authority
  • When you give us explicit permission

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including restricted access, secure storage and regular updates. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

Data retention

We keep project and payment records for as long as needed for support, accounting and legal obligations. ID copies are kept only as long as necessary for verification and records.

Cookies & third-party tools

Our website may use cookies and third-party tools (such as the live chat widget, Google Fonts and embedded YouTube videos) to work properly and understand traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Delete your information (subject to legal and payment records)
  • Stop marketing messages at any time

Children's privacy

Our services are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children; a parent or guardian must place orders on their behalf.

Legal compliance

We comply with India's Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
