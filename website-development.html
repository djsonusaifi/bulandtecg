How much does a website cost in Bulandshahr?

A basic website costs ₹5,000 – 15,000, a business website ₹15,000 – 30,000 and an e-commerce website ₹30,000 – 50,000+. You get a fixed quote after a short discussion.

How long does it take to build a website?

A basic website usually takes 7–15 days. Bigger business and e-commerce websites take longer — your quote mentions the exact timeline.

Will my website work on mobile and show on Google?

Yes. Every website we build is mobile-friendly, fast and set up with the basic SEO Google needs — titles, descriptions, sitemap and clean structure.

Do you make websites outside Bulandshahr?
