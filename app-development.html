FAQ

Common questions.

Still have a question? Visit Help & Support or call us.

How much does app development cost?

A simple app costs ₹20,000 – 40,000, a complex app ₹40,000 – 80,000 and an enterprise app ₹80,000 – 2,00,000+. The final price depends on features.

How long does it take to make an app?

Most apps take 3–8 weeks depending on features, design and backend work.

Will you publish my app on Google Play?

Yes. We have published 7 apps on Google Play and handle the full publishing process. The Google Play developer account fee is paid by the client.

Do you make both Android and iOS apps?

Yes. We build native Android apps and cross-platform apps with Flutter or React Native that run on both Android and iPhone.
