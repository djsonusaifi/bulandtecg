About BulandTech – Digital Agency in Syana, Bulandshahr

A small-town studio with big ambitions.

BulandTech is a digital studio from Syana, Bulandshahr. We help businesses, creators and families across UP and Delhi NCR go online, look professional and grow — with websites, apps, design, video and marketing under one roof.

Our story

Why we started BulandTech.

"Buland" means high, strong and confident — and that is exactly how we want every client's brand to stand. We started BulandTech in Syana because local businesses deserve the same quality of digital work that big-city companies get, at prices that make sense.

Today we design and build websites, publish Android apps on Google Play, create wedding cards, posters and thumbnails, edit cinematic wedding films and run marketing campaigns — all from one team that you can call, message or meet.

BulandTech is founded and led by Sonu Saifi.
