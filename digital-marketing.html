SEO costs ₹5,000 – 15,000 per month, social media marketing ₹8,000 – 20,000 per month and a complete package ₹15,000 – 40,000 per month. Ad budget is separate.

Can you help my business show on Google Maps?

Yes. We set up and optimise your Google Business Profile, local SEO and reviews so nearby customers can find you.

Local SEO usually shows improvement in 2–4 months. Google and Meta ads can bring leads from the first week.
