How do I start a project?

Call or WhatsApp us, or fill the contact form. We discuss your needs, share a written quote, and start once your ID is verified and the 70% advance is received.

What documents do you need before starting?

A valid government photo ID — Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport. Businesses may also be asked for a GST or registration document.

How does payment work?

70% of the quoted amount is paid in advance and 30% before final delivery or go-live. Monthly services are billed at the start of each month. We accept UPI and bank transfer.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope. A basic website usually takes 7–15 days, an app 3–8 weeks, and designs or video edits a few days. Your quote mentions the exact timeline.

How many revisions do I get?

Every quote includes revision rounds. Extra rounds or major changes after approval may be charged separately.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. Bug fixes related to our work are free for the support period mentioned in your quote. Updates and new features after that are charged separately.

Can I get a refund?

If we are unable to start your project, the advance is refunded in full. Once work has started, the advance is non-refundable. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

Who pays for domain, hosting and Play Store fees?

These third-party costs are paid by the client unless they are included in your quote. We help you buy and set them up.

How do I check my project status?

Message us on WhatsApp with your name and project — we share an update the same day.

How can I leave a review?

Contact us for your personal review link. We publish reviews only from verified clients.
